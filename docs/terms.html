Terms of Use

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Pivi Games (“the app”, “we”) is a collection of four classics — sudoku, minesweeper, battleship and ludo — playable solo and, in most of them, online against a friend. By downloading, launching or using the app you agree to these terms. The same text is also available inside the app under Settings → Terms of use, in seven languages.

1 The app

Each game can be played solo, and some also offer an online match for two. There is no account system — nothing needs to be registered to play.

2 Solo play

Solo play works fully offline. Settings and saved games are stored only on your device and are removed together with the app.

3 Online play

To synchronise a match between players, your moves, chosen display name and room code are sent in real time through a third-party cloud service. This data is used only to run the match, is not tied to an account, and is not stored by the developer after the game ends. Do not put personal information in your display name.

4 Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier. Becoming a Supporter (see below) removes ads in all four games.

5 Purchases

The optional “Buy the developer a coffee” purchase is processed entirely by the App Store or Google Play — the developer never receives or stores your payment details. The first purchase unlocks Supporter status (ads removed, cosmetic perks) for good; it is non-consumable and restorable through your store account. Every purchase after that is a consumable tip and is not itself restorable.

7 Acceptable use
