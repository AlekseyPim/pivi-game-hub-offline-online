Frequently asked

How do I start a game? +
The home screen lists all four games. Tap a card to open that game's menu, where you pick your settings and start playing. “‹ All games” at the top-left takes you back to the list.
How does online multiplayer work? +
Inside a game, tap Online → Create room and share the short room code with the other player, who enters it to join. Rooms are per game — a sudoku code will not open a battleship match.
Are settings shared between the games? +
Yes. Theme, language, vibration and your online name are app-wide, so you set them once. Anything that only makes sense inside one game — minesweeper's board palette, battleship's water colours, ludo's fast mode — lives in that game's own settings.
How do I remove ads? +
Tap Buy the developer a coffee on the home screen. It becomes a one-time Supporter unlock that removes ads across all four games for good (and adds a golden victory salute). Everything is completely free without it.
I'm a Supporter but ads came back / I switched phones +
Open Settings → Restore purchases. Your Supporter status is tied to your App Store or Google Play account and will be restored.
I used to play the standalone Pivi apps +
Pivi Games is a separate app, so saved games, settings and Supporter purchases from the standalone Sudoku / Minesweeper / Battleship / Ludo apps do not carry over, and you cannot play an online match between the two. Sorry — store rules give each app its own identity.

What about my data and privacy? +
Solo play works fully offline. For online matches, your chosen name, moves and room code are sent in real time through a third-party cloud service only to run the match — they aren't tied to an account and aren't kept after the game. Please don't put personal information in your display name.
I found a bug or have a suggestion +
Email oleksiipimenov27eu@gmail.com with which game you were in, your device model, iOS/Android version, and what happened. Screenshots help a lot.
